Kevin&Pascaline House Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Kevin&Pascaline House (the app is called UWSlog on your home screen) is a private app for the people who stay at one shared house on the Upper West Side of Manhattan. Access is by invitation and admin approval only. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple we receive your name and your email address (or Apple's private relay address, if you chose to hide it). We do not receive your Apple password.
  • Stay records. The dates you say you are staying at the house, and the names and checkout dates of guests you add. Guests do not have accounts; the name you enter is stored only to show on the roster.
  • Location. The app uses your phone's location for two things:
    1. Noticing, on your phone, that you have entered the Upper West Side, so it can ask whether you are staying at the house. This check runs on your device and the result is not sent to us.
    2. Checking that you are at the house before it shows you who else is staying there. Your coordinates are sent to our server for that one check and are not stored.
    Location is never used for advertising or tracking, and it is not shared with anyone.
  • Notifications. The arrival prompt is generated on your phone. We do not collect advertising identifiers or analytics.

Who can see what

  • Admins can see everyone who is currently staying, upcoming stays, and the occupancy calendar.
  • Other approved members see who is staying only while they are physically at the house.
  • Nothing is public, sold, or shared with third parties for marketing.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple.

How long we keep it

Stay records are kept so the house can review past occupancy. When you delete your account, your sign-in, name, and email are removed and your stays are ended or cancelled. The remaining stay history is kept without your identity, as "Deleted user". Guest names you entered stay in those historical records; email us to have a specific guest name removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Home → Delete account (also on the "Waiting for approval" screen).
  • Location and notifications can be turned off in iOS Settings → UWSlog. Arrival prompts and the roster check will then not work.
  • To ask for a copy of your data or removal of a guest name, email the contact below.

Children

UWSlog is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell members in the app.
