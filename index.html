Kevin&Pascaline House

Kevin&Pascaline House (the app is called UWSlog on your home screen) is a private, invite-only app for the people who share one house on the Upper West Side of Manhattan. It shows who is staying, lets residents add guests and plan stays, and gives admins an occupancy calendar.
